Buy and manage plans on the Omni account hub. Picking a workspace on a pricing card now opens the hub's confirm step for that plan and workspace, and a new workspace is created on the hub's workspaces console. Manage subscription on a workspace's billing page and Manage subscriptions on the profile page open plan management on the account hub, where plans are changed, canceled, or reactivated; all of them come back to Manifold. The in-app checkout, billing portal, and Cancel Plan / Reactivate Plan buttons are removed.
